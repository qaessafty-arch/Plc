## 1. Bag, film and forming-tube geometry

## 2. Machine cycle and theoretical speed (intermittent motion)

| Bag length mm | Pull time s | Peak film speed m/s | Mechanical cycle s | Mechanical limit bags/min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 0.258 | 0.77 | 1.058 | 56.7 |
| 150 | 0.321 | 0.80 | 1.121 | 53.5 |
| 200 | 0.383 | 0.80 | 1.183 | 50.7 |
| 230 | 0.421 | 0.80 | 1.221 | 49.1 |
| 300 | 0.508 | 0.80 | 1.308 | 45.9 |

Cycle = jaw close 0.15 + seal dwell 0.45 + jaw open 0.15 + film pull + settle 0.05 s. Pull uses a trapezoidal profile, a = 6.0 m/s2, vmax = 0.8 m/s [ASM: limited by belt/film friction].

**Product transfer.** Drop from weigh-hopper gate to jaw plane = 1.90 m. Leading-edge free-fall time = sqrt(2h/g) = 0.62 s (air drag ignored, so the real value is longer). Chips arrive as a string; string duration is assumed 0.35 s [ASM - measure with high-speed video]. Tail arrival = 0.97 s after the dump command. The dump is commanded when the jaws start closing, so the tail must arrive before the *next* jaw-close starts, i.e. within one cycle minus jaw-close time: 1.07 s at the mechanical limit -> OK.

Theoretical limit for the reference bag = min(mechanical 49.1, weigher 70, product string 53.5) = **49.1 bags/min**. Rated design point: **40 bags/min** (19 % margin). Validated speed: *none yet*.

One 0.4 kW servo drives both pull belts through a 5:1 planetary gearbox and a cross shaft with two bevel/timing stages, so the belts cannot run at different speeds. The 0.4 kW frame is the smallest V90 PROFINET size and is deliberately oversized: the unknown is film drag, and margin costs little. Inertia ratio must be checked against the motor rotor inertia in the Siemens data sheet (not transcribed here).

Length resolution with the 2500 ppr TTL encoder (x4): 3.8 um of film per count - three orders finer than the +/-1 mm bag-length target, so accuracy is governed by belt slip and registration, not by the drive.

Jaw seal area counts both transverse seals (top of the finished bag and bottom of the next) = 240 x 24 mm. Knife force 150 N is an assumption for 50 um laminate with a serrated blade. Actuators total 123 NL/min; adding 30 % for leakage/tubing and 30 NL/min for the optional bag-deflate/air-blast gives **189 NL/min (0.19 m3/min) at 0.6 MPa**. Specify a compressor of at least 0.28 m3/min FAD with dryer and 0.01 um oil-removal filtration (ISO 8573-1 class 1.4.1 where air can contact product or film inside).

Maximum jaw closing force 1870 N is a crushing hazard: see the safety file - guard interlock, not force limitation, is the protective measure.

Steady state: heat carried away by the film in the seal lands = 116 W at 40 bpm (c = 1900 J/kg.K); convective/radiative loss estimated at ~131 W (3 bars, h = 10 W/m2K, ~0.035 m2 each [ASM]). Installed 1300 W gives a duty cycle of roughly 19 %, which leaves PID authority for recovery after a stop. Each zone has a type-K thermocouple in the jaw, time-proportioned SSR control from the PLC, and an independent bimetal cut-out (manual reset, 250 C) in series with the heater contactor coil.

## 10. Structural checks (first order)

| Check | Value |
|---|---|
| Platform design load (weigher + deck + 2 persons) | 7161 N |
| Leg section | SHS 80 x 80 x 3, 1.4301 (304) |
| Axial stress per leg, load factor 1.5 | 2.9 MPa (yield 210 MPa) |
| Slenderness (K=1) | 64 |
| Euler buckling load per leg, K=2 (unbraced cantilever) | 109 kN vs demand 2.7 kN |

Stress is not the governing criterion; **stiffness is**. A multihead weigher needs a platform whose first natural frequency is well above the feeder excitation and the weigh-cell filter band (suppliers typically ask for > 15 Hz and floor vibration < 0.1 g). The platform therefore gets diagonal bracing on three sides and floor anchors, and the bagger is NOT bolted to it - the jaw impact must not reach the load cells. A modal FEA of the welded platform is an open action before release.
