# Applicable standards and regulations

Intended market stated by the project owner's currency: **Iraq**. Iraq's national standards body is the Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control (COSQC); imported and locally built machinery is in practice specified against IEC/ISO standards and, for exporters, EU directives. **The legally binding national requirements must be confirmed with COSQC or a local conformity body** - this list is an engineering baseline, not legal advice. Standard editions are not quoted from memory; obtain the current edition of each.

| Area | Standard | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Machinery safety - general | ISO 12100 | risk assessment and risk reduction method |
| Packaging machines | EN 415-1 (terminology), **EN 415-3 (form, fill and seal machines)**, EN 415-10 (general requirements) | type-specific hazards and required measures for VFFS |
| Safety-related control | ISO 13849-1 / -2 | PL determination and validation of safety functions |
| Emergency stop | ISO 13850 | E-stop function and devices |
| Guards and interlocks | ISO 14120, ISO 14119 | guard design, interlock selection, defeat prevention |
| Safety distances | ISO 13857, ISO 13854 | reach-through openings (bag exit tunnel), crushing gaps |
| Unexpected start-up | ISO 14118 | isolation and energy dissipation (LOTO provisions) |
| Hot surfaces | ISO 13732-1 | burn thresholds for jaw area |
| Access to machinery | ISO 14122-2/-3/-4 | platform, stair, handrail (1100 mm, knee rail, 100 mm toe board) |
| Electrical equipment | IEC 60204-1 | disconnection, protection, wiring, verification tests |
| LV switchgear assemblies | IEC 61439-1/-2 | cabinet design verification |
| EMC | IEC 61000-6-2 (immunity), IEC 61000-6-4 (emission) | industrial environment |
| Pneumatics | ISO 4414 | pneumatic system safety rules |
| Compressed air quality | ISO 8573-1 | air touching product/film inside: class 1.4.1 or better at point of use |
| Hygienic design | EN 1672-2, ISO 14159; EHEDG guidelines (voluntary) | cleanability of food machinery |
| Food-contact materials (EU reference) | Regulation (EC) 1935/2004, (EC) 2023/2006 (GMP), (EU) 10/2011 (plastics) | declarations of compliance for PP buckets, PU belts, PTFE, seals |
| Food-contact materials (US reference) | FDA 21 CFR 175-178 | alternative supplier declarations |
| Food safety management | Codex CXC 1-1969 (HACCP), ISO 22000 | plant-level; metal detection as CCP |
| Net quantity | OIML R 87 (prepackages), OIML R 61 (automatic gravimetric filling instruments) | weight tolerances; weigher approval |
| Lubricants | NSF H1 registration / ISO 21469 | incidental food contact |
| Noise | ISO 11201 / ISO 3746 | declared emission values (vibratory feeders, air exhausts) |

If the machine is sold into the EU/Türkiye/GCC: Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from January 2027), Low Voltage and EMC directives, technical file, declaration of conformity, CE marking; GCC low-voltage rules (G-mark) for electrical equipment where applicable.
