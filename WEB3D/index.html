    How one bag is made

    One 1.5 s cycle for the reference recipe. Times come from the simulation of the PLC program, not from a machine.

    StepPhaseTime, sWhat happensControlled by
    10Dump request−0.056The PLC tells the weigher to drop the next charge, just before the film pull. It needs about 0.70 s to fall.PLC, weigher
    20Pull film0.000–0.422Belts pull 230 mm of film down over the forming tube.Servo drive
    30Settle0.422–0.474Short pause so the film is still.PLC timer
    40Close jaws0.474–0.596Horizontal jaws and vertical seal bar close.PLC, valves
    50Seal and cut0.596–1.048Two seals at once; knife cuts at 0.798 s. The new charge lands on the closed jaws, 0.646–0.996 s.PLC timer
    60Open jaws1.048–1.170Jaws retract; the finished bag is on the conveyor.Spring-return valves
    10Wait1.172–1.504Hold until the next cycle is due and the weigher is ready.PLC pacing

    Where the project stands

    Done and checked

    • Calculations, layout, 3D model (22 parts), drawings
    • Electrical design, bill of materials (77 lines), budget in USD and IQD
    • Siemens S7-1200 program and HMI design, tested in simulation: 12 scenarios, 51 checks pass
    • Monitoring software, manufacturing, safety and test documentation

    Not yet done

    • Nothing has been built, compiled on a real controller or measured
    • Drawings are layout level, not released for manufacture
    • Weigher and elevator are shown as supplier envelopes
    • Safety must be validated on the built machine by a qualified assessor
